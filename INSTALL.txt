TB Digital Multi Echo - CLAP, VST3 and AU for macOS 11 or later
(Apple Silicon and Intel)

1. Copy each plugin to its folder in your user Library
   (in Finder: Go menu > Go to Folder..., then paste the path):

     TB Digital Multi Echo.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
     TB Digital Multi Echo.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
     TB Digital Multi Echo.clap       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/

   Create a folder if it does not exist. You only need the formats your
   DAW uses: Reaper and FL Studio load VST3 (Reaper also loads CLAP and AU),
   Logic and GarageBand load AU.

2. These plugins are not notarised by Apple, so macOS quarantines them after
   download. Open Terminal and paste this one command to clear that:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"TB Digital Multi Echo.vst3" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"TB Digital Multi Echo.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/"TB Digital Multi Echo.clap"

   (It may report "No such file" for a format you did not copy; that is fine.)

3. Rescan plugins in your DAW (or restart it). The plugin appears as
   "TB Digital Multi Echo" by TB.

Presets are kept in
  ~/Library/Application Support/TB/TB Digital Multi Echo/Presets.txt
and are shared by all three formats.
